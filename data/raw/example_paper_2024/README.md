# Example Paper 2024

This folder demonstrates the paper_key folder structure for organizing extracted data.

## Contents

- `table2_medication_effects.csv` - Effects of methylphenidate on attention and DAT binding
  - Extracted from Table 2 of the published paper
  - Includes both behavior (accuracy) and biomarker (DAT binding) measures
  - Effect sizes reported as Cohen's d with standard errors

## Notes

- Study 1: ADHD population, shows strong behavioral improvement with medication
- Study 2: Control population, minimal effects (as expected)
- DAT binding measured via PET imaging
- All participants completed the continuous performance task at baseline and after medication
